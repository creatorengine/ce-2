Microdramas

For studios and platforms scaling vertical drama

Creator Engine delivers full post-production for microdrama slates, including titles for FOX Entertainment Studios spanning romance, thriller, fantasy, sci-fi and contemporary drama.

Our in-house team brings specialist depth and consistency across titles, helping maintain creative and technical standards with fewer feedback rounds and less required day-to-day post-production oversight for studio teams.

Creator Growth

For established creators and creator-led studios

We run the editing and post-production operation behind creator-led businesses, carrying the format, voice and creative direction into every release.

That model currently supports multiple weekly releases across channels such as food & travel creator Mark Wiens, Paris travel experts Les Frenchies, and celebrity interview show 5 Things with Jay Mewes. These dedicated teams respond to performance, strengthen the work and contribute ideas going far beyond the edit.

Beyond individual episodes, we work with creators to develop repeatable formats and original content IP.

Podcast

For studios, production teams and creators

Conversation-led series shaped through multi-camera editing, sound, graphics and finishing, with the editorial judgment needed to build a distinctive, repeatable format.

We built the editorial and visual format for the Murdoch Mysteries Podcast with Shaftesbury for CBC. For Hizzouse, with Kevin Smith and Jay Mewes, we worked alongside the team as a post and creative partner on the show's style, design, shorts and launch strategy.

Show Marketing

For studios, producers, and marketing teams
360° creative support for shows.

We work in two directions: strengthening the connection with existing audiences and creating new ways for the show to be discovered.

For 5 Things, our digital-first shorts have reached millions of views and introduced the show to new audiences.

The Founders

Built by the people who have led
post-production at scale.

Our founders have spent their careers in film, television and streaming at major entertainment companies. Creator Engine grew from that experience, creating a highly creative team that could evolve with new formats, platforms and release models while helping great stories get told.

Gavin Barclay
Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer
Former Post Production Head, APAC - Netflix · SVP - SIM Digital

Gavin Barclay brings two decades of experience across film, television and digital media. As SVP of SIM Digital’s post division, he oversaw more than 100 films and series each year, including Game of Thrones, Mr. Robot and Pacific Rim. At Netflix, he helped establish its first dedicated APAC post-production team, delivering titles such as Squid Game, Kingdom and Sacred Games.

Samya Bhattacharya
Co-Founder & Chief Growth Officer
Former Nonfiction Post-Production Lead - Netflix · Hotstar

With over 17 years in nonfiction post-production, Samya Bhattacharya began as an editor, working across India’s leading reality and unscripted formats before leading nonfiction post-production teams at Hotstar and Netflix. He set up post-production teams for acclaimed titles including the Oscar-winning The Elephant Whisperers and Filmfare Award-winning The Hunt for Veerappan, as well as House of Secrets: Behind Closed Doors.

Vijay Venkataramanan
Co-Founder & Chief Operating Officer
Former Director of Post Production - Netflix India

Vijay Venkataramanan began his career at DDB Los Angeles before returning to India as a film editor and post-production supervisor. At Amazon, he enabled post teams across Bangalore, Beijing and Chennai to collaborate on campaigns over the cloud. He later joined Netflix India as Director of Post Production, where he led a team delivering over 60 original titles spanning scripted series, feature films and non-fiction.

Across previous roles, the founders have worked on productions including Squid Game, Sacred Games, Delhi Crime, The Elephant Whisperers (Academy Award winner), Suits, MasterChef, House of Secrets and Behind Closed Doors.
