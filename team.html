Our Team

The people
behind the work.

Creative producers, editors, designers, music specialists, colorists, sound engineers, post supervisors and coordinators work together throughout post-production.

Each team is built around the needs of the production, with clear creative, technical and operational ownership at every stage.

Leadership
Sreenjoy Dey
Creative Head, Non-Fiction

Sreenjoy has worked in direction, story, production and show running in unscripted television. As a Reality Head, he worked on formats including The Remix, Fear Factor, MTV Ace of Space and Dance Deewane, with production experience in Spain and Argentina. At Creator Engine, he leads the producers and content teams across non-fiction, shaping the creative approach to client work.

Vikas Acharya
Creative Director, Brands

Vikas began his career as a VFX artist over 17 years ago at a leading boutique post-production studio before becoming a Creative Lead. He has worked on 500+ national TV commercials and motion design packages for major shows on Netflix India, Prime Video and Hotstar, alongside brands including Cadbury, Samsung, Google and Amazon. He now brings that experience to Creator Engine as Creative Director for Brands.

Rupesh Shinde
Head of Creative Operations

Rupesh went from running live events to running post-production. A decade of keeping dozens of moving parts on schedule became valuable training for leading Creative Operations at Creator Engine. He oversees the workflows behind the company’s content verticals for clients around the world, with a focus on scaling operations and processes as the company grows.
